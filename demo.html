Lightkeeper

A read-only glucose display for e-ink screens and bedside clocks, fed from a self-hosted Nightscout site. Built for Kaitlin, shared with the type 1 diabetes open source community.

Everything on this page is simulated in your browser with made-up readings. The real tool needs a Nightscout site and a read-only token (see the README). It is a secondary display, not a medical device: always confirm on the primary CGM app.

  • Never shows old data as current Readings older than 15 minutes, or dated in the future, show as STALE - DO NOT TRUST.
  • Fails loudly, not silently If Nightscout goes down, the last value ages into STALE instead of freezing on screen.
  • Read-only by design It never talks to the pump or sensor. Dosing stays in Trio.
