# Code of Conduct

Lightkeeper is built by and for people living with type 1 diabetes, their partners, and their families. Health is personal and the stakes are real, so this community holds itself to a simple standard.

## Expectations

- Be kind and assume good faith. People arrive here tired, worried, or new to all of this.
- Keep medical questions where they belong. Nobody here gives medical advice; point people to their care team and keep the project's wording about secondary displays intact.
- Protect privacy. Never post real glucose data, Nightscout URLs, tokens, or screenshots containing someone's data. Use the synthetic vectors in `spec/display_vectors.json` for examples.
- Disagree about code, not people. Reviews describe what should change and why; they do not grade the person.
- Take safety concerns seriously and quickly. A report that a display could mislead gets a hazard review, not a debate about tone.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sharing someone's health data, or pressuring anyone to change therapy settings or try an unapproved configuration. Pranks or "tests" involving remote commands are never acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainer at esanacore@gmail.com. Reports are handled discreetly. Maintainers may edit or remove content, and may restrict participation for repeated or serious violations.

This document is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant and the norms of the #WeAreNotWaiting community.
